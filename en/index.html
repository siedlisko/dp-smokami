Dom Pod Smokami Guesthouse in Bukowina Tatrzańska

Located in the heart of Bukowina Tatrzańska, Dom Pod Smokami is an intimate retreat where traditional wooden architecture, panoramic Tatra views, and a peaceful mountain atmosphere create the perfect sanctuary for guests seeking closeness to nature and the authentic vibe of Podhale.
